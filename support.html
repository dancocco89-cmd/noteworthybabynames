Noteworthy Baby Names Support

Help, questions and feedback

Noteworthy Baby Names is the post-it note way for two people to find a baby name they both love. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, email noteworthy.babynames@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Getting started

  • Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • One of you taps Start a new list and shares the 6-character code. The other taps Join their list and enters it.
  • Write names on your own phone. When you've both said yes to a name, it sticks and moves to the Yes pile.

Common questions

My partner's code says no list has that code. Check the letters and numbers (there's no letter O or I, to avoid mix-ups) and make sure your partner hasn't left that list.

I got a new phone. Sign in the same way you did before (Apple or Google) and your list comes straight back.

We're not getting match notifications. Open the app once on each phone and tap Allow when it asks about notifications. You can turn them on later in your phone's Settings, under Noteworthy.

Restoring the unlock. Open Settings in the app, tap Unlock or restore purchase, then Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Deleting your account. In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what's deleted.
